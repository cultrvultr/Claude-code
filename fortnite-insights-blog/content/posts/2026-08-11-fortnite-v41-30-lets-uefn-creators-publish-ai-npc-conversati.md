## What happened

Epic Games' Fortnite **v41.30** ecosystem update (July 30, 2026) expands what UEFN and Fortnite Creative developers can ship — headlined by support for **publishing experiences with LLM-powered NPC conversations**, alongside new mobile creator controls, fresh prefabs and galleries, and an experimental progression API.

## What's new for creators

- **LLM-powered NPC conversations:** creators can now publish islands featuring AI-driven NPC dialogue, opening up new conversational and narrative gameplay.
- **Mobile creator controls:** more control over how islands behave on touchscreen devices, widening reach to mobile players.
- **Shaken Sanctuary prefabs & galleries:** a new asset set (two complete prefabs plus five modular galleries) for laboratories, underground compounds, greenhouses and fortified bases.
- **Experimental Quests API + in-island transactions:** new documentation for in-island transactions and an experimental Quests API that could significantly expand progression systems in creator-made islands.

## Why it matters

AI NPCs and a Quests API push UEFN further toward full-featured game development inside Fortnite, and mobile controls extend the addressable audience. With Fortnite creators having earned a reported $722M across ~260K UEFN islands, tooling that deepens retention and progression is directly tied to the ecosystem's payouts.
