## Summary

**STEAL THE BRAINROT** (island `3225-0366-8885`) held an average and peak of roughly
**22,779 concurrent players** on 2026-08-10, per Chartis' live ReadyUp data. On the same day the
island logged **13,053 daily plays** and **32,869 unique players**, with an average session of
**19.77 minutes**.

Over the trailing 30 days the island accumulated **57,466,709 plays**, contributing to a lifetime
total of **1,471,118,685 plays**.

## Why it matters

Sustained five-figure concurrency paired with a ~20-minute average session is a strong retention
signal for a Fortnite Creative island in the "steal"/brainrot genre — a category that has driven a
large share of rising UEFN maps. High daily unique players relative to daily plays indicates broad
reach rather than a small core of repeat sessions.
