How to play

Play
Starts and stops. Tap or drag the line to jump. Space bar on a keyboard.
Loop, Cues
Loop repeats 4 bars from where you are; tap again to let it run on. Hold Loop to drop a cue at the playhead: a tap near a cue on the line jumps right to it.
Fader
Drag up and down. Double tap for full.
Cut
The stepped filter. Up cuts the bass (high pass), down cuts the top (low pass). Double tap for off.
Echo, Verb
Send the part into the tape echo or the reverb. Double tap for zero.
Throw
Hold to send the whole part into the echo. Let go and the echoes ring on.
Mute, Solo
Tap to drop the part out. Press and hold to solo it: the button turns green and only that part plays for as long as you hold. Let go and everything is back as it was. Hold two at once to hear two.
Knobs
Drag up or down: a short flick turns them all the way. Creep slowly for fine moves. Double tap to reset.
Siren, Synare
The Siren plays while held (set its Trig switch to Toggle on the Siren page to latch it). The Synare hits on each tap. Both play the sound set on their pages. Higher on the Synare button hits harder. The small knob is the level.
Echo
Time, Repeats (past 9 it runs away: drag below 9 to calm it), Return. Tap sets the tempo.
FX
Sync or Free time, width, echo tone, echo into verb, and the reverb's type, decay and cuts.
Mix A B C D
Hold a letter to store the whole mix (faders, sends, cuts, echo and verb). Tap it to bring the mix back, mid-song.
Rec
Records what you hear. Share or save it when you stop.
Pan, Stereo spread
Songs open in stereo: each part is placed left or right by its name (drums, bass and vocals stay in the middle). Turn Stereo spread off in the Song sheet for mono, or move any part with its Pan slider.
Nudge
In the Song sheet, each track has a Nudge in milliseconds to line up parts that were exported a little early or late.
Library
All your songs, the demo first. Tap + Add song and pick one song file: the app asks whether to split it into parts (drums, bass, vocals…) or keep it as one track. Each song keeps its own mix, A–D and takes. The ⋯ next to a song exports, renames or deletes it.
Main menu
Tap the Dub Mixer logo for the main menu: continue the song you are on, or open the Library.
Song
Tap the song title for its settings: title, tempo, stereo spread, and each part's name, pan, nudge and WAV. With more than 4 parts, swipe sideways across the strips to switch between 1–4 and 5–8.
No sound
Volume up. Bluetooth headphones add delay to the pads: use wired or the speaker to play live.
